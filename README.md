CPSC 581 - Group Project 1

A Family of Buttons

Contributers: Adrian Aure, Mark Cawili, Prince Patel, Ayushi Patil

Bubbles is a group project for CPSC 581 Human-Computer Interaction II at the University of Calgary. This project highlights skills of sketching, design, and implementation of a group of 4 buttons. The 4 buttons (bubbles) represent each team member in the group, showcasing their interest/hobbies. Combining the bubbles show similarities in interest for the members as well as their matched ITP results which we have completed as part of class.
